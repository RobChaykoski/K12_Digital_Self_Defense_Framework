## 🔗 How This Fits Our Existing Framework

### Strengths of GECDSB's Approach
1. **Year-round structure** — Not limited to October; provides continuous reinforcement
2. **Family/community focus** — Designed for parents and guardians alongside students
3. **Canadian resources** — Kids Help Phone, Cybertip.ca, Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, Human Trafficking Hotline
4. **Practical checklists** — Actionable tips (VPN, auto-lock, backup plans)
5. **Mental health connection** — Digital wellness, FOMO, sleep, nature prescription
6. **Seasonal relevance** — Back-to-school in September, gaming in April, social media in June/summer

### Gaps Our Framework Fills
1. **Sextortion depth** — GECDSB touches on it in February but doesn't have the "3 Do Nots" protocol
2. **AI forensics** — GECDSM mentions AI generally; we go deep into deepfake detection
3. **Crypto scams** — Not covered by GECDSB at all
4. **Voice cloning / Family Safe Word** — Unique to our framework
5. **Project-based assessments** — Jeopardy reviews, account audits, deepfake labs
6. **Progressive skill-building** — Our phase plans build complexity year-over-year

### Recommended Integration Strategy

```
Week 1-2: Run GECDSB monthly theme as "family discussion" handout
Week 3: Run our Phase Plan session with hands-on activity
Week 4: ECNO-aligned lesson from k-12cyberawareon.ca
```

This gives schools a **3-source rotation**:
- **GECDSB** → family/community engagement
- **Our framework** → deep skill-building
- **ECNO** → Ontario-wide campaign alignment
