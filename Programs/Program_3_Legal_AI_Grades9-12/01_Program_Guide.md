# 🎓 Program 3: The Digital Frontier — Legal & AI (Grades 9–12)

## Overview
The **Wild West phase**. Students now face adult-grade threats with adult consequences: credential-stuffing operations, app-based MFA and recovery architecture, audio-forensic deepfake analysis, sextortion as an organized business model, crypto scams (rug pulls, fake airdrops, pump-and-dump), algorithmic manipulation, and the **legal landscape** — what's actually illegal in Canada (Criminal Code: extortion s.346, publication of intimate images s.276, fraud, identity theft) and where their rights sit as victims.

|---|---|
| **Grades / Ages** | 9–12 / 14–18 |

## Learning Objectives
By the end, students will be able to:
1. Run a personal credential audit and eliminate password reuse; explain **credential stuffing** and breach databases.
2. Implement **app-based MFA** (authenticator apps), set up recovery options, and explain why SMS is weaker.
3. Analyze cloned audio and lip-sync video using forensic tells; verify identity before acting on any digital request.
4. Dismantle a **deepfake** using at least 4 technical tells + free detection tools (reverse image search, metadata, detector apps).
5. Explain sextortion as an organized business model (syndicates, scripts, payment rails) and apply the full victim protocol including legal reporting options.
6. Identify Canadian law that applies: extortion, intimate image publication, fraud, identity theft — and what "consent" means digitally.
7. Evaluate **crypto scams** (rug pulls, fake airdrops, pump-and-dump, influencer shilling) using a red-flag framework; explain why "recovery agents" are second-layer scams.
8. Analyze how algorithms create echo chambers and engagement bait; choose feeds deliberately.
9. Execute a multi-platform incident response in the **Wild West Simulation**.
10. Navigate resources: Cybertip.ca, Take It Down, NeedHelpNow.ca, Project Arachnid, and when police involvement is warranted.

## Differentiation
- **Grade 9:** scaffolded forensic checklists; pair with a Grade 11/12 buddy in mixed classes.
- **Grade 12 / OSSD-linked:** extend S8 into a personal-finance ethics unit; S6–7 can feed OSSLT-style civics analysis of the Criminal Code sections.
- **ELL students:** all handouts use the same icon system as Programs 1–2 (🚫/✅/⏳); forensic labs are visual-first.
- **No-device days:** every lab has a paper walkthrough version (screenshot packs).

## Safeguarding Notes
- S6–7 require the counsellor; use the Program 2 sensitive-block protocol (clinical language, anonymous slips, no personal disclosure aloud).
- Crypto content is **defensive only** — no trading platforms, no wallet setup, no token names promoted. Frame: "here's how the scam works so you can spot it."
- Deepfake material must be public/celebrity-labelled; never classmates. AI-CSAEM discussed at pattern level only — never shown.
- Legal content is informational, not legal advice; route specific situations to counsellor/police.
