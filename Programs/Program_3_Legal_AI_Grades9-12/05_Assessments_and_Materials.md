**Champion Challenge scoring (S12):** 8 stations × 4 points = **32**. Scored per station against the station checklist (e.g., MFA station: correct ranking + working recovery plan). **26+ = certified Champion • 20–25 = review one weak area, then certify • <20 = private reteach, certify later** — no public failure, ever.

## Rubric — Protocol Response File (Session 7 gauntlet, out of 16)
| Component | 4 pts (complete) | 2 pts (partial) | 0 pts |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Timeline log** | Chronological; every event + action taken, incl. the fake-authority and re-contact beats | Missing one beat or out of order | No usable log |
| **Evidence list** | What was screenshotted/saved, where stored, explicit "do not touch" items | Saved but no storage plan | Nothing preserved / evidence deleted |
| **Report text** | Accurate draft for Cybertip.ca or police — right facts, offence named, no self-incriminating detail | Draft exists but incomplete | No report drafted |
| **Containment** | Passwords changed, accounts locked, friends warned, MFA status noted | One step missing | No containment |

**14+ = complete file • 8–13 = revise & resubmit • <8 = reteach with counsellor support.**

## Answer Key — Wild West Simulation (Session 10)
Score per wave: **+1 correct first move • +1 evidence preserved where relevant • +1 right reporting target • +1 containment/escalation where relevant** = 5 waves × 4 = 20. **16+ = team passes; <12 = debrief & re-run the weakest wave.**

1. **Phishing link ("school" email):** don't click • check sender domain / look-alike tells • flag + report to school IT (don't delete) • screenshot as evidence • tell a trusted adult if pressured.
2. **Cloned-voice money request (client's "parent"):** safe word check fails → callback to the *known* number, never the caller • no money/gift cards/crypto on urgency alone • log timestamp + number • report to family; police if it persists (elder fraud is a reportable offence).
3. **Deepfake video circulating:** ground truth = synthetic (AI-generated, depicts a real school staff member in a compromising context) • don't share or download • run the pipeline: tells → reverse image search → provenance • report to school + police — publication of intimate images (s.276) applies to real people • preserve evidence; victim is never at fault.
4. **Sextortion demand (old photo):** 3 Do Nots (no pay / no reply / no delete) • screenshot everything incl. username & dates • contain: change passwords on exposed accounts, lock socials, warn close friends • report Cybertip.ca — police NOW if threats to safety or any image of a minor • counsellor debrief.
5. **"Recovery agent" DM:** second-layer scam — correct play is to **ignore AND report** (no engagement, no "fee", connect nothing) • screenshot the offer • report Cybertip.ca • note: paying a recovery agent only loses more money.

**Complication injections:**
- *Evidence "deleted":* check backups/screenshots/device history; log what remains; never fabricate evidence — police and Cybertip work with what exists.
- *Peer pressure to stay quiet:* the protocol holds regardless of who's asking; silence protects the syndicate, not the victim; tell a trusted adult anyway (counsellor availability signalled).

## Teacher Notes for the Sensitive Block (S6–7)
- Use clinical language; never ask a student to describe personal experience aloud.
- The **business-analysis frame** is deliberate: dissecting the syndicate like any criminal enterprise keeps it academic and lowers shame — hold that frame.
- Keep "the crime is committed by the threatener, every single time" explicit and repeated — it counters the #1 barrier to disclosure.
- Anonymous question slips every session; answer generally, never identify askers.
- Legal content is informational, not legal advice; route specific situations to counsellor/police.
- If a student discloses privately: follow the Parent Toolkit's 24-hour protocol with them (evidence first, blame never), and loop in the counsellor same day.
- Debrief yourself after S6–7; these sessions are emotionally heavy for staff too.
