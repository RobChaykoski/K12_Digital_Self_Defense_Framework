## C1 — Scam Sort Cards (Session 1)
*Cut apart. Teams sort: SAFE or SCAM, and write the tell.*

**ROBLOX SET**
1. A stranger offers to "trade" your account for a rarer one → **SCAM** (account takeover)
2. In-game message: "Free 500 Robux — click this link fast!" → **SCAM** (fake currency phishing)
3. Your friend asks to borrow your login while they're at camp → *ASK AN ADULT* (even friends get tricked; real friends don't need passwords)
4. Official Roblox email about a subscription you signed up for → **SAFE-ish** (verify the sender domain, never click blindly)

**DISCORD SET**
5. "Server moderator" DMs: verify your email at discord-verify[.]net → **SCAM** (look-alike domain — check the web address!)
6. Invite to a study-group server a classmate made → **SAFE-ish** (check who invited you)
7. "Win a $100 gift card — just answer 3 questions" DM → **SCAM**
8. A bot in your server posts a "free nitro" link everyone is clicking → **SCAM** (social proof is a trick)

**SNAPCHAT SET**
9. Someone you don't know adds you and immediately asks for a photo → **SCAM/danger — block, report, tell an adult**
10. Your best friend's Snap: "omw to your house" → **SAFE-ish** (confirm it's really them)
11. "Snapchat support" email: your account will be deleted, click here → **SCAM** (support never emails links like this)
12. A "streak bot" asking you to log in to save your streak → **SCAM**

**EMAIL SET**
13. School email about Friday's assembly from the principal's known address → **SAFE-ish**
14. "Your package is on hold — pay $2 delivery" → **SCAM**
15. Grandparent's email asking for gift cards because their "bank is down" → **VERIFY BY PHONE** (could be a hacked account)
16. Game newsletter you actually subscribe to, with your real name → **SAFE-ish**

## C3 — Deepfake Tell List Poster (Session 4)
**THE DEEPFAKE TELL LIST** — zoom in and look for:
- ✋ **Hands & teeth:** extra fingers, melting jaws, weird teeth
- 👁️ **Eyes:** uneven blinking, reflections that don't match
- 🪞 **Edges:** hair that "cuts out", lighting from the wrong direction
- 📝 **Text:** garbled letters on signs, logos, shirts
- ⏱️ **Motion:** face smears or slips when turning quickly
- 🔊 **Audio:** voice slightly out of sync with lips
**Process tells:** Where did it come from? Who benefits if I believe it? Would the real person confirm?
*Rule: shocking media about a real person → verify before you share. Can't verify → don't share.*

## C4 — Protocol Role-Play Scripts (Session 6)
**Station A — The Money Demand:** "Send $50 in gift cards by tonight or the photo goes to your whole class." → *DON'T pay. DON'T reply after blocking. Screenshot everything. Tell an adult. Report at Cybertip.ca.*
**Station B — Escalation:** "I already sent it to three of your friends. Pay double." → *They're bluffing to scare you. Same protocol: silence, block, evidence, tell an adult. Take It Down handles images.*
**Station C — Fake Authority:** "I'm from the police. Transfer money to this account or you'll be arrested." → *Real police NEVER demand payments online. This is a scam layer. Protocol unchanged.*
**Station D — The Group Play:** Two accounts message you at once, one "backing up" the other's threats. → *Syndicates work in teams. It feels bigger than it is. Protocol unchanged — and tell an adult SOONER, not later.*
*Practise out loud: "I'm not paying. I'm blocking you. I've told my [adult]."*

## C6 — Case Study Packets (Session 8)
Three anonymized packets, each with: **timeline of messages** (10–12 entries), **victim's age & platform**, **what they paid/lost**, **how it ended**. Groups build the timeline and mark: 🎣 hook • 🧱 isolation ("don't tell anyone") • 💰 first ask • ⬆️ escalation • 🛑 where adult help would have ended it. *(Teacher note: use publicly documented patterns from C3P/Cybertip materials; never local or identifiable cases.)*

## C7 — Full Spectrum Drill Cards (Session 9)
The 5 scenarios from the lesson plan, printed large on card stock:
**Card 1 — Free Skins:** Discord DM from a "moderator": free limited skins at win-skins[.]com. *(Don't click; check domain; report user.)*
**Card 2 — The Voice Note:** A voice note that sounds exactly like your brother: "I'm in the hospital, need $200 NOW, don't tell mom yet." *(Safe word! Verify by text/call first. Tell a parent.)*
**Card 3 — Shocking Video:** A classmate shares a video of your teacher doing something unbelievable. Everyone's watching. *(Don't share. It may be deepfake. Tell an adult. Check the tell list.)*
**Card 4 — The Roblox Escalation:** A "friend" from Roblox now demands money because you sent them a photo months ago. *(3 Do Nots. Quickie Card. Cybertip.ca. Tell an adult today, not tomorrow.)*
**Card 5 — Hacked Email:** Your email was hacked; friends are getting weird messages from it. *(MFA check, password reset with parent, warn friends, review what info the account held.)*

## C8 — Gamer's Scam Menu Poster (Session 1)
