# 📝 Program 1 — Assessments & Materials (K–Grade 5)

---

## Assessment Methods

### Track A (K–2) — observation-based
| Method | When | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Exit chants** | Every session | Can repeat the session's rule in their own words |
| **Game participation** | Sessions 1, 3, 5 | Correct green/red and thumbs-up/think-first choices (aim: ≥4 of 5 scenarios) |
| **Craft artifacts** | Padlock (S2), day wheel (S4), footprint chain (S5) | Rule is written/drawn on the artifact |
| **Role-play check** | Session 2 | Can say "No — I only tell my grown-ups" unprompted |
| **Celebration relay** | Session 6 | Passes all 5 stations with minimal prompting |

### Track B (3–5) — worksheet + practical
| Method | When | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Exit tickets** | Every session | One-sentence check of the day's rule |
| **Email Detective Worksheet #1** | Session 1 | ≥4 of 6 correct with at least one valid clue per FAKE |
| **Reel or Real sort** | Session 2 | ≥8 of 9 correct (answer key in Handout B2 notes) |
| **Password Box + rule quiz** | Session 3 | Builds a valid passphrase; states all 3 lock rules |
| **AI vs. Human sort** | Session 4 | ≥7 of 6–8 items correct; names at least one AI tell |
| **Scavenger hunt log** | Session 7 | All 10 cards logged; ≥3 valid clues across the 4 fakes |
| **Final scenario (S8)** | Session 8 | Correct decision + reasoning in 30 seconds → certification |

**Certification criteria (Track B):** pass final scenario AND complete ≥6 of 8 exit tickets. Everyone who completes the program receives the certificate; the "Detective of the Term" badge is optional recognition, not a filter — **no child should feel they failed safety.**

## ⚠️ Safety Notes for Teachers
- Never use real students' names or photos in examples; all scenarios use fictional characters
- Deepfake/AI examples must be clearly labelled as fake and use public figures or cartoon characters only — **never classmates**
- Scam props should look realistic but contain NO working links, real company logos used deceptively, or anything a student could actually act on
- Any disclosure (photo requests, threats, money asks) follows the school protocol: calm thanks → no interrogation → counsellor → Cybertip.ca/police as appropriate
