## Appendix A: "The Internet Playground" (sample story, K–2)
Mia loves her tablet. One day a message pops up: *"Hi! I'm 8 too and I have a secret game code. Tell me your password and I'll share it!"* Mia remembers the playground rule: **online, anyone could be anyone.** She doesn't type anything. She runs to her mom. Mom says: *You did the bravest thing — you told me.* They report the message together. Mia's padlock stays locked, and she gets a sticker on the Green Button wall.

## Appendix B: Mystery Message Envelope contents (3–5)
Each envelope contains one prop message + a "trick checklist" sheet:
1. **The Free Robux** — urgency + asks for account info
2. **You Won a Prize!** — too-good offer + asks for parent's card number
3. **Your Account is Deleted** — fear + fake login link
4. **A Friend's New Number** — looks friendly, but the "friend" you've never met online asks for a photo
Trick checklist: ☐ rush me ☐ scare me ☐ ask for info ☐ ask me to click ☐ too good to be true
