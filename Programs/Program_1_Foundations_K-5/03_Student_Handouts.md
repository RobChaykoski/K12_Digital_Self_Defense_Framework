## Handout A1: My 5 Safety Rules (K–2 cape page)
*(Students complete this on their superhero cape in Session 6)*

1. Online, anyone could be **anyone** 🎭
2. My password is my **key** — only my family knows it 🔒
3. I tap the **GREEN** button and tell an adult about RED ones 🟢🔴
4. Screens are a **snack**, not the whole meal ⏰
5. Before I post, I think: **will I be okay with this in 10 years?** 👣

## Handout A2: Mask Match Cards (K–2, cut apart)
**GREEN cards (safe to talk to):** Mom / Dad / My teacher / Grandma / A family friend your parents know
**RED cards (tell a trusted adult first!):** Someone online who says they're my age / "A nice person" in a game chat / Anyone asking for my password, photo, or where I live
*Game: teacher reads a scenario — hold up GREEN or RED!*

## Handout B1: Email Detective Worksheet #1 (3–5)
**Your job:** Circle the clues. Stamp each message **REAL ✅** or **FAKE 🚨**. Write at least one clue for every FAKE.

1. *From: school@ourschool.ca — "Library book due Friday. See you there!"* → ______
2. *From: win-notice@prizes4u.com — "CONGRATULATIONS!!! You are the 1,000,000th visitor! Click NOW to claim your $500 gift card before midnight!"* → ______ Clue: ____________
3. *From: mom@gmail.com — "Can you pick up milk after school? Also, I've moved my email, click here to log in to my new account."* → ______ Clue: ____________ *(tricky one!)*
4. *From: teacher@ourschool.ca — "Reminder: picture day is Wednesday. Forms are in your folder."* → ______
5. *From: robux-giveaway@free-stuff.net — "FREE 10,000 Robux! Just tell us your username and password so we can add them to your account!"* → ______ Clue: ____________
6. *From: grandpa@yahoo.com — "Hey champ, it's me! I'm at a hotel and my phone died. Can you send me $20 on your mom's card? Tell no one."* → ______ Clue: ____________ *(account-takeover scam!)*

**The 3 Clues of a Fake:** (1) rushes or scares me ☐ (2) asks for info or clicks ☐ (3) sender looks slightly off ☐

## Handout B2: Reel or Real? Card Sort (3–5, cut apart)
**Sort each card: REAL (happened as shown) / REEL (edited, selected, or fake).**
1. A "perfect beach day" photo with skin that looks airbrushed → ______
2. A classmate's video of their dog falling off the couch → ______
3. An influencer's 5 a.m. gym post — but the caption admits it was take #9 → ______
4. A friend's birthday party where only 2 friends show up in every photo → ______
5. A "before/after" weight-loss ad with no real person shown → ______
6. Your neighbour mowing their lawn, filmed by a kid with a shaky phone → ______
7. A celebrity video that looks slightly too smooth and says something they'd never say → ______ *(AI!)*
8. A group chat screenshot shared without asking the people in it → ______ *(real image, unfair use!)*
**Rule to remember:** Compare yourself to highlight reels and you'll always lose — it's not a fair game.

## Handout B3: Password Box Word Cards (3–5, cut apart)
**Pick 3 words at random. Join them with dashes + add a number or symbol. That's your passphrase!**
`purple / toast / rocket / mountain / guitar / banana / winter / pencil / dragon / ocean / cookie / lightning`
**Example:** `Purple-Toast-42!`
**The 3 Lock Rules:** 1) Never reuse a lock for two doors (no reusing passphrases) 2) Only your family holds the key 3) If you think someone saw it, tell an adult to change it

## Handout B4: AI vs. Human Sort Sheet (3–5)
**Mark each item AI-made 🤖 or human-made ✋ — then check the answer key with your teacher.**
1. A drawing of a cat with seven toes → ______ *(AI — extra fingers/toes are a classic tell)*
2. Your friend's messy homework doodle → ______
3. A sentence: "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog and then sits down to have a sandwich by the river." → ______
4. A voice reading your name with slightly robotic rhythm → ______
5. A photo of your school where the flag has nonsense symbols on it → ______ *(AI — flags/logos/text often come out wrong)*
6. A handwritten letter from grandma with a smudged word → ______
**The tell list:** extra fingers ☐ weird text/flags ☐ too-smooth faces ☐ sounds slightly off ☐ **Rule:** see/hear it ≠ real it.

## Handout B5: Mystery Message Envelope — Trick Checklist (3–5)
For each envelope message, tick every trick you find:
☐ Rushes me ("NOW!" "before midnight!") ☐ Scares me ("deleted!" "suspended!")
☐ Asks for my password or info ☐ Asks for a parent's card number
☐ Asks me to click a link ☐ Too good to be true ☐ Pretends to be someone I know
*Then design your WARNING LABEL sticker and stick it on the message.*

## Handout B6: Link Lab Cards (3–5, paper version — cut apart)
**Hover-Check before you click! Read the REAL address. Mark CLICK ✅ / DON'T 🚫 + one reason.**
1. Link says "School lunch menu" → real address: `lunch@ourschool.ca` → ______
2. Link says "Free Robux" → real address: `fr3e-r0bux-b4it.net/go/xyz` → ______
3. Link from a friend says "look at this video" → real address: `youtube.com/watch?v=...` → ______ *(still ask the friend first!)*
4. Link says "Your email is full" → real address: `mail-secure-login.ru/inbox` → ______
5. A text from an unknown number: bit.ly/xyz → ______
**The one question:** *Do I actually know where this link goes — and who sent it?*
