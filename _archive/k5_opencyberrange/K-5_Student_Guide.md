# K-5 Cyber Adventures: Student Guide

## Welcome to Your Digital Adventure! 🌟

Hello, young explorers! 

Welcome to the **K-5 Cyber Range** - where you'll become digital detectives and cybersecurity heroes! Every challenge is an adventure waiting for you to solve.

## How to Start Your Adventure

1. **Login** to your teacher's account
2. **Find your first mission** in the K-5 track
3. **Launch your lab** using the Start button
4. **Follow the instructions** to complete your challenge
5. **Capture your flag** when you solve the mystery!

## Your Adventure Tools

### Basic Commands (Don't worry, we'll guide you!)
- `ping` - Check if a computer is awake
- `nmap` - Look for what services are available
- `curl` - Get information from websites
- `cat` - Read files and find clues

### The Treasure Hunt Process
1. **Discover** - Find where to look
2. **Explore** - Examine the area 
3. **Find** - Locate your treasure (flag)
4. **Capture** - Submit your success!

## Getting Help When You Need It

### Hints Available
If you get stuck, don't worry! We have helpful hints that will guide you:
- Start with the first hint immediately
- Later hints appear as you work through the challenge
- Hints explain what to do next in simple terms

### Your Teacher is Here to Help
If you're having trouble:
1. Ask your teacher for assistance
2. Review the instructions carefully
3. Try the hint system to get guidance

## What You'll Learn

By completing these adventures, you'll become a cybersecurity expert who can:
- Find information on networks safely
- Understand how computers communicate 
- Protect digital treasures from others
- Think like a detective solving mysteries

## Success Tips for Young Explorers

### Be Patient and Persistent
Some challenges might take time. That's okay! Learning takes practice.

### Think Like a Detective
Look for patterns, clues, and connections between what you see.

### Ask for Help When Needed
Don't be afraid to ask your teacher or classmates for assistance.
