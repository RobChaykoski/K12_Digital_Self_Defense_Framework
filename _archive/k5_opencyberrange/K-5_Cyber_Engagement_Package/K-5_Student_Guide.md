# K-5 Cyber Adventures: Student Guide

## Welcome to Your Digital Adventure!

Hello, young explorers! 

Welcome to the K-5 Cyber Range - where you'll become digital detectives and cybersecurity heroes! Every challenge is an adventure waiting for you to solve.

## How to Start Your Adventure

1. **Log In**: Use your teacher's login credentials to access the platform
2. **Choose Your Challenge**: Select from our exciting cyber adventures
3. **Start Exploring**: Follow the instructions to complete each mission
4. **Find the Flag**: Discover the hidden treasure (flag) in each challenge!

## Your First Adventure: Treasure Hunt

Ready? Let's begin your first adventure! You'll need to find the treasure map hidden in our cyber range. 

Remember: Every expert was once a beginner. Good luck, young explorer!
