## Proposed Framework Reorganization

### Current Structure
```
Program 1: Foundations (K-5)
Program 2: Social Dynamics (6-8)
Program 3: Legal & AI (9-12)
```

### Recommended Structure
```
8 Permanent Strands (each K-12):
1. Identity & Authentication
2. Privacy & Data
3. Devices & Networks
4. Social Engineering
5. AI Literacy & Defense
6. Media Authenticity
7. Incident Response & Resilience
8. Ethics, Law & Citizenship
```

### Recommended Phase Reorganization
```
K-2 → Awareness and Trust
3-5 → Digital Identity and AI Awareness
6-8 → Cybersecurity, AI and Manipulation
9-10 → Systems, AI Risk and Digital Evidence
11-12 → Cyber Resilience, AI Governance and Career Readiness
```

## Research Opportunity

The public.txt identifies a publishable education-research question:

> **"What cybersecurity and AI self-defense competencies should every student possess by the time they graduate from secondary school, regardless of whether they pursue a career in computing?"**

This distinguishes the framework from:
- **Professional tracks:** "How do we prepare future cybersecurity professionals?"
- **Infrastructure tracks:** "How do schools protect their infrastructure?"

The unique intellectual contribution:
> **"What does an ordinary 18-year-old need to know to defend their identity, data, finances, reputation, and decision-making in an AI-mediated world?"**

## The Big Picture
The current framework is **strong on foundations** but needs **3 major structural upgrades** to align with 2026 K-12 cybersecurity + AI education directions:

### 1. AI Must Start at Kindergarten
- Current: AI introduced at Grade 3
- Required: AI awareness from K-2 ("Computers can make mistakes")
- Separation: AI Literacy (how it works) vs AI Security (how it attacks)

### 2. Add Permanent 8-Strand Model
- Replace program-only structure with 8 cross-cutting strands
- Each strand progresses K-12
- Better academic organization

### 3. Fill 4 Critical Gaps
- **Threat modelling** (Grades 6-12)
- **Full incident response** (STOP-DOC-TELL-PROTECT-RECOVER)
- **Data/algorithmic literacy** (inference, profiling, aggregation)
- **Agentic AI security** (AI permissions, autonomous actions)
