## 🗺️ What Is "AI for All"?

ISED's **AI for All** is Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy portal. It represents the federal government's comprehensive approach to AI leadership, covering:

### Pillar 1: Pan-Canadian AI Research Strategy
- Investment in AI research institutes (Mila, Vector, Amii)
- Academic-industry partnerships
- Regional AI hubs across Canada

### Pillar 2: AI Global Leadership
- International AI standards and collaboration
- Canada's role in global AI governance
- Cross-border data flow policies

### Pillar 3: Responsible AI Development
- Ethical frameworks for AI deployment
- Trust, safety, and accountability in AI systems
- Transparency requirements for AI-generated content

### Pillar 4: AI Adoption Across Sectors
- Government AI implementation (G7 Canada.AI)
- Industry AI integration
- SME and startup AI support

### Pillar 5: AI Skills & Talent Development
- Workforce AI literacy programs
- Education pipeline from K-12 through university
- Inclusive AI education (Indigenous, rural, underserved communities)

### Pillar 6: Indigenous AI & Data Sovereignty
- Integration of Indigenous knowledge systems in AI
- Community-controlled data governance
- Traditional ecological knowledge in AI models

### Pillar 7: AI for All — Accessibility
- Making AI tools available to all Canadians
- Digital divide reduction
- Multilingual AI tools
- AI in public services

## 🇨🇦 Specific Canadian AI Context Additions

### For Elementary (K-5):
- **Mila AI Institute** (Montreal) — "Canada's AI brain" — named after Dr. Yoshua Bengio
- **Vector Institute** (Toronto) — AI for healthcare and business
- **Amii** (Edmonton) — AI for industry and energy
- **Canada.AI** — how the federal government uses AI
- **G7 Canada.AI Process** — Canada led global AI policy discussions in 2023-24

### For Middle School (6-8):
- **Pan-Canadian AI Strategy** — $125M+ in government investment
- **AI & Data Act (AIDA)** — Canada's proposed AI legislation
- ** OECD AI Policy Observatory** — Canadian contributions to global standards
- **Indigenous AI Governance Principles** — CPTPP AI guidelines for Indigenous data

### For High School (9-12):
- **Canadian AI Strategy 2020** — Government's 5-pillar national strategy
- **AIDA (Artificial Intelligence and Data Act)** — Regulatory framework for high-risk AI
- **Global AI Governance Hub** — Canadian contributions to UN AI governance
- **AI Ethics in Canadian Law** — How Criminal Code sections interact with AI (extortion, fraud, deepfakes)
- **Canadian AI Startup Ecosystem** — From HAIL Health to Deep Genomics
